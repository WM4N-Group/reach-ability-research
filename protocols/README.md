# Small frozen research protocols, not datasets or execution authority

These are byte-preserved copies of the existing future-new-data high-level critic purpose grant/protocol and the two-condition native-prefix DEV audit protocol. They keep intent and stopping rules available after a compute-node switch. They contain no credentials, raw observations, GT, weights or model artifacts.

No data is included. Copied authority/purpose text does not grant new physical execution, validate a new machine, change any closed scope, or promote DEV/audit material to training. Paths/source pins in the historical files remain literal provenance. Do not rewrite a frozen protocol in place merely to suit a new server; any actual owner-qualified change must be a newly identified version.

Source hashes are in SOURCE_PINS.json. Current repository README/operations documents supersede historical machine-placement descriptions but do not relax the original experiment/safety/data-role rules.
